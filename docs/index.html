OYUN VERİSİ NEREDE SAKLANIYOR

Oyun ilerlemen — açtığın bölümler, yıldızların, ışığın, temaların, dekorasyonların, başarıların ve ayarların — telefonunda saklanır.

Bu veriler bize gelmez. Bizim sunucumuz yok.

Google hesabına yedekleme

Telefonun, Android'in standart yedekleme sistemiyle bu verileri **senin kendi Google hesabına** yedekler. Amaç şudur: telefon değiştirirsen veya telefonu sıfırlarsan oyun ilerlemen kaybolmasın.

Bu yedek:

  • Senin Google hesabındadır, bizim değil
  • Google'ın sunucusundadır, biz erişemeyiz
  • İçinde sadece oyun ilerlemesi vardır, kişisel veri yoktur

Bu yedeklemeyi kapatmak istersen: Telefon Ayarlar → Google → Yedekleme bölümünden kapatabilirsin. Bu, telefonundaki tüm uygulamalar için geçerlidir.

Bu isteğe bağlıdır. Giriş yapmadan oyun tam olarak çalışır. Hiçbir bölüm, özellik veya içerik girişe bağlı değildir.

Girişi geri almak için: telefonundaki Play Oyunlar uygulamasından oyunu hesabından kaldırabilirsin.

SHORT VERSION

Prismio collects no data from you. We have no server. Nothing reaches us. The game works offline. The internet is used only for the optional Google Play Games sign-in.

DATA WE COLLECT

None.

Prismio does not collect:

  • Name, email, phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts, photos, files
  • Device identifier, advertising identifier
  • Usage statistics
  • Crash logs

WHY THE INTERNET PERMISSION

Prismio requests the INTERNET permission only for Google Play Games.

The game itself (puzzles, map, menus) never connects to the internet. This is also enforced technically: the game screen cannot make any external request, the app blocks it.

If you do not sign in, the app connects nowhere.

WHERE GAME DATA IS STORED

Your progress — unlocked levels, stars, light, themes, decorations, achievements and settings — is stored on your phone.

This data does not reach us. We have no server.

Backup to your Google account

Your phone backs this data up to your own Google account using Android's standard backup system. The purpose is simple: if you change phones or reset your phone, your progress is not lost.

This backup:

  • Is in your Google account, not ours
  • Sits on Google's servers; we cannot access it
  • Contains only game progress, no personal data

To turn this backup off: phone Settings → Google → Backup. This applies to every app on your phone.

Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is linked to your Google account and opens on another phone as well.

This is optional. The game works fully without signing in. No level, feature or content is locked behind sign-in.

If you sign in:

  • Your progress is saved to Google's servers, in your account
  • Google gives us a player identifier. It is a number; it does not contain your name or email
  • We cannot access this data. We have no server

This feature is also covered by Google Play Games' own privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

To undo the sign-in: remove the game from your account in the Play Games app on your phone.

Manual transfer

Inside the game, under Settings → Account and backup, you can get a code. That code stays with you; we never see it.

PURCHASE (PRISMIO PLUS)

Prismio has a single optional purchase: Prismio Plus. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription.

The purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Your payment details go directly to Google. We never see, store or have any access to your card details.

The app receives only one piece of information from Google: whether this purchase was made, yes or no. That answer is stored on your phone.

Google Play Billing is subject to Google's own privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

THIRD PARTY SERVICES

There are two services, both from Google:

  • Google Play Games — optional sign-in and cloud save
  • Google Play Billing — only if Prismio Plus is purchased

Not present in Prismio:

  • No ad network
  • No analytics service
  • No crash reporting
  • No social media connection
  • No external fonts or images are loaded

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Prismio is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data from any user of any age group, there is no specific risk to children.

YOUR RIGHTS OVER YOUR DATA

Since we collect no data, there is nothing of yours for us to delete, correct or transfer.

To delete the game data on your phone, uninstall the app.

To delete the backup in your Google account: Google Drive → Settings → Manage backups and remove the Prismio backup. That backup is in your account; we cannot access it.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is renewed. If advertising or Google Play Games sign-in is added to the app, this policy is updated beforehand.
